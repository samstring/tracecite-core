Version: 1.2.8 gamma Rev. 383, x86_64-Linux-qt5, Ubuntu 26.04

Symptom
=======
Viewing or editing a reduced Swagger YAML file with F3/F4 reproducibly crashes Double Commander with EAccessViolation. The reporter noticed the YAML uses backslash-escaped line endings and that the viewer renders nonsense around those lines before crashing.
