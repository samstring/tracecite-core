Source: https://github.com/doublecmd/doublecmd/issues/2777
Project: doublecmd/doublecmd
Version: 1.2.3 beta Rev. 178, x86_64-Linux-qt6, Arch Linux/KDE Wayland, Qt 6.10.2

Symptom
=======
Opening Options -> File Associations closes Double Commander. Deleting extassoc.xml makes the settings section open again; after creating an association and reopening the section, it crashes again. Existing associations otherwise work. Version 1.1.32 worked on the same OS.

============
