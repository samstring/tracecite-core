Source: https://github.com/prometheus/prometheus/issues/18018
Project: prometheus/prometheus
Reported against: Prometheus v3.8.1 and v3.9.1

Symptom
=======
Several PromQL range-function queries panic when an @ modifier points to a timestamp with no data.

Observed error:

execution: unexpected error: runtime error: index out of range [0] with length 0

Expected behavior: return no data instead of panicking.

Reproduction queries
====================

quantile_over_time(scalar(up) + 1, {__name__="up"}[1h:1m] @ 1111111)
deriv({__name__="up"}[1h:1m] @ 1111111)
changes({__name__="up"}[1h:1m] @ 1111111)
predict_linear({__name__="up"}[1h:1m] @ 111111111, 0.1)

The reporter notes that data exists at ordinary timestamps but the chosen @ timestamp has no data. The same family of queries without the empty @ result does not panic. The reporter also suspects a behavior change after Prometheus PR #16797.

Important contrast
==================
The failing @ timestamp produces an empty range/vector. Queries with data at the evaluation point, or equivalent queries without the empty @ selection, do not show the panic.
